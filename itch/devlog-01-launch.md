Hi! SLAMLINE is my arcade racing game, and today it's on itch.io. It's free, it runs in your browser, and there's a Windows download if you'd rather have it as an app.

The idea is simple: drive fast, fill your boost, and slam the other cars into the walls. You charge boost by drifting, slipstreaming and scraping past rivals. Then you spend it to catch up and take someone out. Every takedown plays in slow motion, and some have names: pin a rival against the wall for a GRINDER, ram one from behind for a SHUNT, or land on top of one from a jump.

[image: screenshot-1-space.png]

**Ten tracks, each with its own trick**

I didn't want ten tracks that only look different, so each one has something you can't find on the others:

- Neon City: a car-park spiral you drive up and jump off the roof
- Alpine Pass: a ski jump over a ravine
- Whiteout Summit: a frozen lake where your tyres barely grip
- Skyport: a jet taking off on the runway next to you
- Magma Core: a loop-the-loop inside a volcano
- Nebula Ring: a low-gravity gap above a ringed planet

Every track also hides two or three shortcuts. Some AI drivers know them too.

[image: screenshot-4-alpine.png]

**Rivals and power-ups**

Whoever wrecks you becomes your RIVAL, and taking them out gets you PAYBACK boost. Some AI drivers are bullies who ram you, some sneak through every shortcut, and some race clean. You find out which is which on the road.

There are also power-up blocks on the road: Shockwave, Ricochet, Lightning Strike and Oil Slick.

[image: screenshot-3-canal.png]

**Play with friends**

- **Split screen** for two players on one keyboard or two gamepads
- **Online rooms** for up to 8 players: one person hosts and gets a 6-character code, the others type it in. No accounts.
- **Phones and tablets** work too, with touch or tilt steering.

**Tell me what you think**

This is the first public version, and I'm still working on it. If something breaks, feels unfair or is just fun, tell me. Leave a comment here, or use the FEEDBACK button on the title screen. If you find a bug, RECORD in the pause menu saves a video of your race, which helps me a lot.

Thanks for playing!
